# Project Discovery

Language for exploring real projects and finding inspiration for what to build.

## Language

**Project**:
A concrete software or hardware build, including applications, developer tools, and libraries. Tutorials, resource lists, and starter templates are outside the catalog.

**Domain**:
A field of application, such as healthcare or fashion, in which a project is used.
_Avoid_: Industry (as a separate concept)

**Problem space**:
A grouping of projects addressing related problems, such as medication adherence or outfit visualization. These groupings emerge from the projects in the catalog; a project can belong to multiple problem spaces.
_Avoid_: Domain, technology (as synonyms)

**Technology**:
A technical method or tool used by a project, such as computer vision. It is distinct from the problem addressed and the domain of application.

**Subcategory**:
A problem space narrower than a parent problem space. A space may have multiple parents.

**Recent prominence**:
The number of distinct catalog projects in a problem space, including its subcategories, published within the last 90 days. It describes observed recent publication volume, not growth over time or complete ecosystem activity.

**Publication date**:
The earliest known publication or announcement date for a project across its sources, with its source and date type retained. Repository creation is a labeled fallback when a public publication date is unavailable.
